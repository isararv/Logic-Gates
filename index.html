AFTER PUZZLE 36

Meet hexadecimal: base 16

Decimal has ten digits, 0–9. Hex has sixteen: 0–9, then A–F. Each hex digit represents exactly four bits.

Base 10Base 16 / HEXBinary

For eight bits, use two hex digits. The left digit is worth 16 times its value: 2F = 2 × 16 + 15 = 47 = 00101111₂. The full eight-bit range is decimal 0–255, or hex 00–FF.
